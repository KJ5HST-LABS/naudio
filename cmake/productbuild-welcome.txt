Network Audio Service streams audio from a capture device on this Mac to applications that listen for it over the network, and lets you set that up from a page in your browser.

This installer places the service, its control page and the naudio command-line tools on this Mac. The service is listed in System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions and starts at your next login; it opens no microphone until you press Start on its page.

Read Me on the next pane says where everything goes and how to remove it.
